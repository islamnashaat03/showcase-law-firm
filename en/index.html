Common consultation questions

A few things you might like to know before starting a conversation.

What happens in an initial consultation?
For this demo, the consultation request simply prepares your chosen topic and contact details locally. In a real practice, an initial conversation would establish the matter, the appropriate team and the proposed scope.
How is the scope of work agreed?
The sample process allows space to clarify objectives, responsibilities and proposed fees before any engagement. No engagement or adviser relationship is created by this demonstration.
Can you work with existing advisers?
The demonstration practice is designed around a collaborative approach. A real engagement would agree permissions and responsibilities before information is shared.
What should I include in the request?
Use a name, email and broad practice area to try the form. Do not include confidential documents, detailed personal circumstances or sensitive information. Nothing is sent.
